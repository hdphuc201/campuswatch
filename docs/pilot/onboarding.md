# CampusWatch pilot: getting started

CampusWatch tells you when your grades, tuition, timetable, exam schedule, or Dashboard
notices change on the CITD Portal. Thank you for testing it.

## Before you start

- You need Google Chrome on your own computer.
- CampusWatch checks the Portal **only while Chrome is running**, about every 30 minutes.
  When Chrome or your computer is off, nothing is checked.
- CampusWatch **never asks for your Portal password**. You log in to the Portal yourself,
  as usual. If anything asks for your Portal password inside CampusWatch, stop and tell us.
- Portal reading is in **beta**: if a notification looks wrong, check the Portal directly and
  let us know.

## Install (pilot version)

1. Download the CampusWatch zip file from the pilot organizers and unzip it into a folder you
   will keep (for example `Documents/CampusWatch`).
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and switch on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
4. Pin CampusWatch: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to CampusWatch.

## Set up

1. Click the CampusWatch icon and **Create a CampusWatch account** (an email and a new
   password of at least 12 characters — not your Portal password).
2. Read the consent screen and press **I agree, connect**.
3. Open the CITD Portal in Chrome and log in. Within a minute the popup shows **Connected**.
   Your current data is recorded first; you are only notified about changes after that.
4. Optional: **Settings and data** lets you choose which changes notify you, set quiet hours,
   choose summary or detailed messages, and link Telegram.

## Everyday use

| You see                     | What it means                                                           |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connected · Last checked …  | All good.                                                               |
| Log in to the CITD Portal   | Your Portal session ended. Log in again in Chrome; CampusWatch resumes. |
| Cannot reach CampusWatch    | Internet or server problem; it retries automatically.                   |
| CampusWatch needs an update | The Portal changed its layout; we are fixing it.                        |
| Paused                      | You paused monitoring. Press **Resume** when ready.                     |

**Sync now** checks immediately (at most once every 5 minutes).

## Your data and privacy

- Stored: the grades, tuition, timetable, exam, and notice data CampusWatch reads, and their
  change history (up to 12 months).
- Never stored: your Portal password, cookies, or the Portal pages themselves.
- **Disconnect Portal** stops monitoring. In **Settings and data → Your data** you can download
  everything we store, delete your data, or delete your account at any time.

Full details: the CampusWatch privacy policy (pilot draft).

## Updating

When we send a new version, unzip it over the same folder (or a new one) and press the reload
icon on CampusWatch in `chrome://extensions`. You stay signed in.

## Help

Contact the pilot support channel. Never send your Portal password or screenshots that show
your grades or tuition amounts.
